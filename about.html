About Hostel Connect Banda

A simple student-focused project designed to make the accommodation search clearer and more organised.

Our Purpose

Finding a suitable hostel can be stressful, especially when a student is new to Banda or Kampala. Hostel Connect Banda brings key accommodation information into one easy-to-navigate website.

The project helps users understand what to compare and how to make careful enquiries before choosing a place to stay.

Our Priorities

  • Clear and accessible information.
  • Student comfort and wellbeing.
  • Responsible accommodation decisions.
  • Simple communication between students and providers.

Who Can Use the Website?

Designed for people involved in the student accommodation journey.

🎓

Students
